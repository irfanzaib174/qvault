What is post-quantum cryptography?

Score: 0

Security learning center

Open a topic to learn the core idea. These lessons are educational, not a security audit.

Post-quantum cryptography refers to cryptographic algorithms designed to remain secure against attacks from future quantum computers as well as conventional computers. Standardization and implementation are ongoing; using the term alone does not make a wallet quantum-resistant.
A recovery phrase may give someone control over the assets associated with a wallet. Never share it with a person, website or support agent. QVault will never ask you for a recovery phrase or private key.
Smart accounts use programmable logic to manage transactions. Depending on their design, they may support features such as sponsored fees, spending limits or multiple approval steps. These features do not automatically guarantee security or post-quantum protection.
Phishing attempts trick people into revealing secrets or approving harmful actions. Check domains carefully, avoid unexpected links, and never approve a transaction you do not understand.
